    Why Forgehouse?

    We started with one idea: a serious gym shouldn't feel intimidating. So we built a place with proper equipment, honest coaching and a community that pushes you without pressuring you.

    From strength and conditioning to yoga and group classes, everything here is designed to help you build habits that last, not just a few good weeks.

    Our mission

    Make good training accessible, safe and enjoyable for everyone who walks in.

    Our vision

    A fitter, stronger, more confident neighbourhood, one member at a time.

    • Certified trainers
    • First-aid trained staff
    • Member-first coaching
